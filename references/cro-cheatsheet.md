# CRO cheatsheet - when clicks don't convert

The ads are working if people click. If they click and don't call, book or submit, the PAGE is the problem.
Next: work this list top to bottom - each item is bigger than the ones below it.

---

## 1. Match the page to the ad (the biggest leak)

The page's H1 repeats the ad's promise word-for-word. Someone who clicked "Emergency Drain Cleaning Dallas" must land on a page that says exactly that, not "Welcome to Smith Plumbing." A mismatch means an instant back button AND a worse Quality Score, which means higher cost per click. One landing page per ad group exists for exactly this reason.

## 2. Put ONE call to action above the fold

A single visible action before any scrolling: call button on mobile, or a short form on desktop. Not three options. Not a nav bar full of exits. Every extra choice costs conversions. Repeat the same call to action after every proof block down the page.

## 3. Load in under 2 seconds on a phone

Every extra second of load time drops conversions measurably. Compress images to WebP, kill unused scripts, no popups. Test on a real phone over cellular, not your office wifi.

## 4. Design the phone version first

Most local service clicks are phones. Thumb-reachable call button, form fields large enough to tap, no horizontal scrolling, click-to-call number in the header. The desktop version is the adaptation, not the starting point.

## 5. Stack proof where doubt happens

- Review stars plus the exact count near the call to action, because doubt peaks at the moment of action
- Real photos of team, trucks and jobs. Stock photos read as fake and convert like it
- License and insurance line in the hero area
- Guarantee restated next to the form

Every proof item must be real and confirmed by the owner - ask before you write it.

## 6. Cut the form down

- 3-4 fields maximum: name, phone, what do you need. Every extra field cuts submissions
- Button text says the outcome, never "Submit". Use "Get My Free Quote"
- The thank-you page sets the expectation ("we call within X minutes") and fires the conversion tag

## 7. Kill the remaining friction

- Answer the top 3 objections in an FAQ: price, timing, guarantee
- Show the service area so out-of-area visitors self-select out
- No "we're passionate about excellence" filler. Specifics only

---

## Read the numbers to find which problem you have

**Lots of clicks, no conversions**
This file, top to bottom.

**Good conversion rate, junk leads**
The traffic source is wrong, not the page.

**Few clicks at decent impressions**
The title and meta description. Run `/seo-optimization meta`.

**Few impressions**
Bids, budget or keyword volume. `/audit` finds which.

Deep CRO - A/B testing, heat maps, session recordings - is its own discipline. This cheatsheet is the 80/20 that fixes most pages without any tooling.
