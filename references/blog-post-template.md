# Blog Post Template - THE locked structure

Every blog post `/blog-post` writes uses THIS skeleton. The research and voice change per post; the structure never does. It bakes in [on-page-seo.md](../references/on-page-seo.md) and [geo.md](../references/geo.md) so every post ships citation-ready without retrofitting. Built once, reused forever - if the structure needs improving, improve THIS file, and every future post inherits it.

---

## The skeleton (section order is fixed)

### 1. Title + meta
2-3 variants per [meta-info.md](../references/meta-info.md) formulas, primary keyword front-loaded, 50-60 chars. H1 = the primary keyword phrased the way the searcher typed it.

### 2. The Quick Answer (directly under the H1 - NON-NEGOTIABLE)
40-60 words that fully answer the primary question, self-contained, liftable verbatim. Include the key number. This is what AI cites and what the featured snippet takes - 44% of citations come from the first 30% of the page, so the answer goes FIRST.

### 3. The proof line (1-2 sentences)
Why listen to us: the strongest relevant credential + number from proof-inventory ("We've replaced 400 water heaters since 2011 - here's what they actually cost."). Establishes first-hand experience for E-E-A-T before the content starts.

**⛔ NEVER write a disclaimer about proof you do not have. BANNED, no exceptions.**

Not as a preamble, not as a footnote, not "read this before you trust anything below", not "we have not published a result for X yet", not "we are not going to borrow somebody else's", not "every number here is ours, sourced, or labelled as an estimate". A reader who arrived from Google does not know what you lack until you tell them, and opening by listing your gaps is the single least persuasive thing a page can do. It reads as an apology, it plants doubt before the first sentence of value, and no publication on earth does it.

The honesty rule means **never claim a result you do not have.** It has never meant announcing the absence. If the proof is thin, write the proof line from what IS true and strong, and say nothing about the rest:

- Wrong: "We have not published an SEO result for a home services company yet, and we are not going to borrow somebody else's."
- Right: "We grew a local service business to 1,500 leads a month and sold it, and we sit in front of 400+ owners every week."

Same facts. One apologises, one is a credential. Sourcing and estimate labels belong inline next to the number they qualify, never gathered into a confession at the top.

### 4. The main table
One comparison/pricing/spec table near the top covering the core data of the topic (real numbers from proof + research, sources cited). Tables earn ~25% more AI citations - every post has at least one; pricing topics get 2-3.

### 5. Body sections - one H2 per cluster secondary + the merged outline
- Each H2 phrased as the question people search
- Answer-first inside every section: direct answer, THEN the depth, story, or humour
- Cluster secondaries from keyword-map.md each get their H2; spec outline fills the rest
- Weave in: 1 real story from voice/stories.md, stats with original sources, at least one expert/customer quote with attribution
- One idea per section; ~18-word average sentences; no fluff transitions

### 6. The "what we see in the field" section (the information gain)
The section no competitor can copy: OUR jobs, OUR prices, OUR before/afters from proof-inventory. First-person, specific, numbered. This is the #1 citation lever (original data) and the E-E-A-T anchor.

### 7. FAQ (4-6 questions)
The real People Also Ask + spec questions, phrased as searched, answered in 2-3 tight sentences each.

### 8. The bridge CTA
2-3 sentences linking to the relevant SERVICE page - the money path. Blog traffic must have somewhere to convert. Descriptive anchor, never "click here". Plus the hub link (up) per the internal-linking rules.

### 9. Author box
Real name, photo, one-line credential from proof-inventory. Person schema attached.

### Schema (invisible layer, every post)
Article + Person (author) + Organization, validating clean. FAQ content stays in the HTML.

### Images
Real ones from proof/images first; every image WebP, descriptive filename + alt, width/height set, lazy below the fold.

## Rules

- **The skeleton is locked.** Sections 1-9 in this order, every post. A post may EXPAND a section, never delete or reorder one. (Exception: no real proof for section 6 yet = say so honestly and use the best true experience available - never fabricate.)
- **Voice on top, structure underneath.** Humour and personality live INSIDE the sections (especially 5) - the reader feels the voice, the machine sees the structure. The bar is the humour rule in CLAUDE.md: a standup set - nearly every line entertains, specificity is the joke, real stories beat invented bits. Exception zones kept straight: the Quick Answer and FAQ (AI lifts these verbatim - clean and direct).
- **Improve the template, not the instance.** Found a better pattern? Update THIS file (and note the date below) so every future post gets it. One-off deviations die with the post.
