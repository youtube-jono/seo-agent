# Build your citation directory listings

The safe starting list of directories to list the business on, in the order to do them. Updated Wednesday 13 August 2026.
Next: lock the NAP format below, then submit Tier 1 (10 listings, about 3 hours).

**What a citation is.** Your business listed on a third-party directory with its NAP: Name, Address, Phone. Google cross-references these to verify the business is real. Consistent NAP across 30 to 50 directories beats messy NAP across 300.

---

## The golden rule: NAP must be byte-identical everywhere

Pick one format and never deviate.

- **Name** - exact spelling, no extra "LLC" on some and "Inc" on others
- **Address** - pick "St" OR "Street", never mix
- **Phone** - pick `(416) 555-0142` OR `416-555-0142`, never mix
- **Website** - pick `https://www.example.com` OR `https://example.com`, never mix

One inconsistency and Google treats it as a different business. Fifty inconsistencies and your local pack ranking tanks.

These are gold but require local research. Run `WebSearch "[city name] business directory"` and look for:

- Regional tourism board listings

- **Toronto** - BlogTO Best Of, Toronto Life, Narcity, BIA listings
- **New York City** - Time Out NY, Yelp NYC, Citysearch
- **Los Angeles** - LA Weekly, Time Out LA, Eater LA for food
- **London** - Time Out London, Hotfrog UK, 192.com

### Do it manually - free, time-intensive

Submit each directory by hand. About 5 to 10 minutes per listing times 30 listings is 3 to 5 hours in total, at no cost.

### The hybrid, recommended for solos and small businesses

1. Manually submit Tier 1 and Tier 2, the 25 most important, all free
2. Run a SEMRush Listing Management scan to find gaps and submit to the long tail
3. Manually submit Tier 4, the industry-specific ones - these matter more than the long tail
4. Set a quarterly reminder to re-check NAP consistency

---

## What NOT to do

**Never submit to 500+ random directories.** Spam citations dilute the signal and some get penalized.

**Never use different NAP variations.** "Acme Co" on one and "Acme Company Inc" on another confuses Google.

**Never pay for "1000 citations for $50" services.** They submit to garbage sites and can trigger a manual review.

**Never skip the data aggregators.** Tier 3 saves more than 50 hours of manual work.

**Never ignore industry directories.** Industry-specific authority beats 50 generic citations.

---

## Quarterly maintenance checklist

Every 3 months, run this:

- [ ] WebSearch your business name plus city, check the first 2 pages of results
- [ ] Check NAP consistency on the top 10 listings, look for drift
- [ ] Update hours if seasonal
- [ ] Add any new locations to ALL directories
- [ ] Remove duplicate listings, Google penalizes duplicates
- [ ] Re-run the SEMRush Listing Scan if subscribed
